# WORK-0001 — MiniDoc initial development contour

**Status:** active

## Purpose

Develop the commissioned small Windows document application to the most complete honestly verified state achievable in the available environment, while preserving a cold-recoverable engineering record in this repository.

## Subject and boundary

The Subject is the target Windows desktop product and all repository-local engineering state required to research, define, design, implement, build, install, verify, document, integrate, and close that target.

Included: problem-space Research, supported DOCX/PDF boundaries, Word-like but bounded desktop editing UX, text editing/search/replace, direct formatting, paragraph controls, editable tables, explicit chart/diagram handling, Windows GUI/platform choice, dependency boundary, file/state lifecycle, build, install/uninstall, source implementation, documentation, verification evidence, and closure. Shape support, footnotes, TOC and similar popular functions are preference-driven candidates whose admission depends on research and a coherent small-product boundary.

Excluded unless later explicitly admitted: use of other user repositories/workspaces as content or architecture sources; third-party product/runtime libraries or document engines; full Microsoft Word/Acrobat parity; PDF editing; background helpers/services/updaters; speculative future features without a current driver.

## Baseline

Product, project Knowledge, Evidence, and workspace were absent at Commission start. The repository itself was empty. A first v0.1 target/design/plan was formed and partial source implementation began before `INBOX-0002`; that new input materially invalidates the affected DOCX/UI target and plan slices while leaving repository history and unaffected platform/lifecycle research as valid evidence candidates.

## Allowed effects

Read current MADARAII governing materials; perform public external research; create/update files and commits in `ForestTiger-GH/madaraii-pilot-3`; create the Product and its build/installation scripts; produce verification procedures and evidence.

## Prohibited effects

Mutating `MADARAII`; using MADAR, Compass, other pilot repositories, other project workspaces, or their accumulated knowledge as project input; introducing third-party product/runtime dependencies; claiming Windows runtime/install behavior as executed when it was only statically inspected; silently treating a stale pre-`INBOX-0002` target as current.

## Completion

The contour may close when: both raw inbox carriers are preserved and developed; materially required Research is assimilated; Target WHAT/HOW are current and sufficient for the expanded authoritative requirements; a coherent Product candidate is implemented; build/install/uninstall and user/engineering documentation exist; available verification is complete and Windows-only checks are reproducibly specified; Product state and limitations are truthful; all material Questions/residue have an owner or terminal disposition; cold recovery works.

## Stop / reopen

Stop a dependent slice on unresolved Commission, mandatory owner ambiguity, prohibited dependency need, a material design defect, or human scope change that invalidates its baseline. Reopen affected scope on a material human requirement change, governing revision change, failed Windows verification, or evidence that invalidates an admitted target/design claim.
